# Shared MQTT log queue (BROTLib#138, #139, #130)

Status: implemented in `fix/log-queue-138-139`; offline tests only, **not run on the telescope** (IAG50cm test plan T44).

## Problem

T44 steps 3 and 4 (broker stopped for 10 s and 45 s during a startup, IAG50cm 0.5.0 with BROTLib 0.14.0):

- Stage lines (`PowerOn progress`, `GOHOME progress`) never reached `50cm/Log`, with no ADS warning and no readable counter (#138).
- The lines that did arrive came up to 12 s late and `IAG50cm startup finished` was ahead of `GOHOME commencing` (#139).

## Cause (read from code and the issue logs)

- Every lost line comes from an `FB_EventLog` declared in a method's `VAR` in IAG50cm `FB_TelescopeControl` (`GoToEvent`, `HomeEvent`, `ParkEvent`, `PowerOnEvent`, `SlewEvent`). Method `VAR` is new on every call, so the pending slot, the attempt counter and the retry timer are gone when the method returns. A first publish that failed was forgotten, `_GiveUp` never ran, `nNotDelivered` stayed 0.
- The late lines come from member instances, each with its own 1 s retry timer; the order across instances is not kept.
- `PublishLog` used `bQueue := TRUE`, i.e. relied on the Tc3_IotBase client queue: finite, size undocumented, nothing readable about what it refused.

## Change

- `FB_LogQueue`: ring buffer of 128 finished log lines, oldest first; a full queue pushes out the oldest line.
- `FB_Comm_MQTT_Influx.PublishLog` formats and truncates the line as before and only pushes it (returns TRUE = accepted). `_DrainLog` (called from the body after `SUPER^()`) sends up to `nLogPerCycle` (5) lines per cycle, oldest first, only while `_LogLinkUp()` (connected and subscribed), with `bQueue := FALSE`. A refused line stays at the head and is tried again, so the order never changes.
- Counters: `logQueued`, `logQueuedMax`, `logLost` (new); `logFailed` now counts refused sends (the line is kept); `logDropped` unchanged (line too long). Lines never sent = `logLost` + `logDropped`.
- Overflow: rate-limited ADS warning (1st and every 100th), and one `WARNING` line on the log topic after the queue is empty: `MQTT log: n lines lost, ...`.
- `FB_Comm_MQTT`: ADS hint `MQTT connected to <host>` and warning `MQTT connection lost, log lines are queued` on the transitions, so a test can compare timestamps.
- `I_Comm` unchanged except the `PublishLog` comment. `FB_EventLog` code unchanged (its retry stays for a Comm that can refuse a line).

Decided: drop the oldest line on overflow; no event-time Influx timestamp on log lines (needs the consumers checked first); reconnect delay not tuned (client `ActivateExponentialBackoff` defaults unknown).

## Not covered

- A dead TCP connection that the client does not notice yet (QoS 0, `nKeepAlive` 60 s): lines sent in that window can still be lost. Hardware test T44 step 3b.
- The queue is not task-safe: `PublishLog` must be called from the task that runs the comm.
- The client's reconnect delay (11.7 s after the 10 s outage in #139) is not changed here.
- Other repos may have `FB_EventLog` in method `VAR` too (not searched); it is delivered correctly now either way.
